Free calculators for everyday decisions

EasyTools helps you calculate percentages, discounts, profit margins, tips, loan payments, compound interest, averages and more. Enter your numbers, check the result, and keep your work moving without creating an account.

How to calculate a percentage

To find a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. EasyTools does the arithmetic for you.

How to calculate profit margin

Profit is selling price minus cost. Profit margin is profit divided by selling price, expressed as a percentage.

How to calculate a discount

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage to find the savings, then subtract the savings from the original price.
